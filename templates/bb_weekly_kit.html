Step 2

Mailchimp email

Mailchimp can't attach files; the download buttons link to the PDFs online. Pick one way to set the links:

Option A (quickest): reuse the blog's PDFs. On the published post, right-click each click here → Copy link address and paste it below. The copied email will already have working buttons.

Option B: upload from your computer in Mailchimp. Leave the boxes blank. After pasting the email, click into the email body in Mailchimp, select each download button, choose the link option → File, and upload the matching PDF.
