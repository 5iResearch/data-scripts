Fear breadth

How many of the indexes are in their fear zone. Since 2011, the S&P 500's average return over the following year has been about +14% with none or one, +22% with two or three, +28% with four or five and +49% with six or more.

0–1: normal   2–3: favourable   4 or more: highly favourable

Where the fear is

Whether the unease is in stocks, commodities or both. A group counts as uneasy when at least two of its indexes are at their uncertain level or higher. Nervous stocks with calm commodities has been the weakest backdrop for the S&P 500: the market worrying about itself, as in late 2021 and early 2022. Unease in both at once has been the strongest.
