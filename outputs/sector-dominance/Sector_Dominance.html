Sector Head-to-Heads

Prices as of November 06, 2026 · which stocks have consistently beaten which, inside each sector

A stock can lead its sector over ten years on the strength of one great run. This page asks a stricter question: how often has it come out ahead? Every stock is matched against every other stock in its sector, once for each 12-month window starting at a month-end over the past ten years, and we count who had the better return. A name that wins most of those match-ups has outperformed its peers steadily, not just once.

Treat it as a record of the past, not a forecast. When we tested it back to 2011, a high score did little to predict the next 12 months in the US and nothing in Canada, where mining and energy leaders tend to swap places with each commodity cycle.

Beat the sector: the stock's average win rate against its sector peers (50% is average). Dominates: peers it beat in at least 70% of windows; Dominated by: peers that beat it that often. Median / worst 12 mo vs sector: its 12-month return minus the sector median, typical and worst window. short marks stocks with under five years of history, judged on fewer windows.

Source: 5i Research, Yahoo Finance. Total returns (dividends reinvested). Canada: TSX stocks over C$1 billion; US: S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100. Today's members only, so stocks that dropped out over the decade are missing.
