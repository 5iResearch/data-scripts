Breadth Divergence Map

Where each theme sits on relative strength vs participation
How to read it. The rotation dashboard tells you how stretched a theme's leadership is. This tells you how broad it is — and narrowing usually comes first.
  • RS participation is the one to watch: the share of constituents whose own ratio to the benchmark is above its own 200-day MA. When the basket's RS line is still rising but RS participation is falling, a handful of names are carrying the theme and the rotation out has already started underneath.
  • Breadth composite = mean of (% above 50dma, RS participation). A Negative divergence means RS rose over 13 weeks while breadth fell more than 5 points — leadership narrowing, top risk. Positive is the mirror: RS still falling but breadth improving, which is what the end of a washout looks like.
  • Narrow high is the stricter version: the RS line is at a 26-week high while the breadth composite is not. Rarer, and a more pointed warning.
  • Shaded bands on each chart mark historical divergence runs of two weeks or more. Scroll back through them to see how much lead time they actually gave — that is the question the lead-lag study will answer properly.
  • Breadth is a warning, not a trigger. It says the leadership is fragile, not that it has broken. Pair it with the 13-week ROC cross on the rotation dashboard.
