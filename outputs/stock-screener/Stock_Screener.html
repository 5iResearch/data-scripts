To add tickers to the frontier, re-run this workflow manually with a comma-separated ticker list in the "extra_tickers" input. Every "as of" snapshot uses today's S&P 500 / NASDAQ-100 / TSX constituents (no point-in-time index membership source) evaluated with a 10yr trailing price window ending on that snapshot's date — i.e. what each metric would have read on that date, not a reconstruction of who was actually in the index then.
