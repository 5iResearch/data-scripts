Step 2

Mailchimp email

This goes to subscribers, so it has the PDF download buttons. Mailchimp can't attach files; the buttons link to the PDFs online. Pick one way to set the links:

Option A: upload to Mailchimp first. In Mailchimp, Content → Content Studio → Upload both PDFs, copy each file's link and paste it below. The copied email will already have working buttons.

Option B: link the buttons inside the email. Leave the boxes blank. After pasting the email, click into the email body in Mailchimp, select each download button, choose the link option → File, and upload the matching PDF.
