Finding your way around

What's on the home page?
  • Our Current View: our market view in one line.
  • Market Memes: what the market consensus is currently saying.
  • Section cards: Market Musings, Stock of Interest, Portfolio Risk Lab and Chart of Interest. Hover over a card for a short description, or click to open it.
  • Investor Suite Watchlist: company, ticker, price, forward P/E, yield, market cap and our comments.
  • Suite Screens: top-five lists refreshed with each monthly update, covering 52-week highs and lows, the largest changes in insider positions, short interest, dividends and target prices.

A disclosure sits at the bottom of the page.

How do I read a full article?

Click a section card to see a summary of the latest piece with its chart, then click Open Full Market Musing, Open Full Research Note or Open Full Chart of Interest. The full piece opens across the whole page. Click the X at the top right to close it.

How do I read the Stock of Interest cohort table?

Each row is one Stock of Interest from the year's cohort:

CompanyThe company and its ticker.
Price at ReleaseThe share price when we published the report.
Target PriceOur target price in the report.
Current PriceThe latest share price.
Release DateWhen the report was published.
Confidence in Target Being ReachedOur confidence that the target will be reached. Once a stock hits its target, this shows Target price achieved on [date].
Research ReportClick View Report to read the full report.

Prices at release are as of each report's release date.

Portfolio Risk Lab

What is the Portfolio Risk Lab?

Twelve interactive charts and tools in three groups. Each chart opens with a short explanation of what it shows.

Market Conditions
Where the market stands in its long-term cycle
  • 200-Week Moving Average
  • Index RSI Report
  • Macro Market Model: Canada
  • Macro Market Model: US
Investor Sentiment
Fear, greed and risk appetite
  • Volatility Index (VIX) Report
  • Offense vs. Defense
  • Levered ETF Sentiment
Stock Tools
Research individual stocks
  • Stock Lookup Tool
  • Sharpe Ratio
  • Sector Head-to-Heads
  • Regression Channel Screener
  • Revenue Revision Screener
How often is it updated?

Most charts update every weekday overnight. Sector Head-to-Heads updates weekly, on Saturdays. Each chart shows its as-of date under its title.

How do I use the charts?
  • Switch charts from the list on the left, or step through all 12 with the arrows at the top right.
  • Change the time frame with the YTD, 1Y, 3Y, 5Y, 10Y and All buttons.
  • Zoom in by clicking and dragging across the chart. Double-click to zoom back out.
  • Hover over a line to see its values on that date.
  • Full screen: Stock Tools pages have a Full screen button. Press Esc to exit.
What can I do with Stock Lookup?

Enter a ticker or company name to see a stock's long-term trend, what analysts are doing with their revenue forecasts, how its risk and return compare with the rest of its market, and whether it has beaten simply owning the index.

It covers stocks in the TSX, S&P 500 and Nasdaq 100. Canadian tickers end in .TO, for example RY.TO, SHOP.TO or CNQ.TO; U.S. tickers are entered as they are, such as AAPL, NVDA or JPM. Results show the last price, the percentage above or below the 200-week average, the one-year return, volatility and the Sharpe ratio.

Are the charts buy or sell signals?

No. They're tools for understanding market conditions, sentiment and individual stocks, and they work best alongside our research and your own judgment.

Some are deliberately contrarian. The Macro Market Model, for example, tends to read most positive when news and sentiment are at their worst. Past patterns don't guarantee future results.
